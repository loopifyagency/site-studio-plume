# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Assumed (not asked): plain static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployable to any static host. GSAP + ScrollTrigger and Lenis are vendored locally in `assets/vendor/`; fonts are self-hosted. Reason: the brief names GSAP/ScrollTrigger/Lenis, a Google Maps iframe, JSON-LD and `tel:` links, all of which a static page carries. Deploy target unknown.

## Users

Women (stated target) living in or near Brest who want a facial or lymphatic massage and decide on the strength of a few photos, honest prices and other clients' words. They arrive from Instagram, Planity or a search for "massage Brest", mostly on a phone. Inferred: many are booking a first session or a gift.

## Product Purpose

Studio Plume - Clémence Autret is a massage institute at 46 Rue de Lyon, 29200 Brest. The site exists to get a visitor to book an appointment (main action: "prendre rendez-vous", through Planity or by phone). Secondary actions: call, offer a gift card, find the studio.

## Positioning

A one-practitioner studio, not a spa chain: the hands of Clémence Autret, a short menu of three signature treatments (Kobido, lymphatic drainage with the Vodder method, and both together), a lit neon sign reading "Studio Plume" as the studio's own landmark.

## Product Principles

1. Show real hands, real prices and real words; nothing in the page is made up to look bigger.
2. Booking is one tap away from every screen (Planity button, floating "Appeler" on mobile).
3. The studio is a warm, lit room: the page should feel like walking into it, not like browsing a catalogue.
4. Motion serves the calm of the treatment: slow, eased, never jittery; it steps aside for users who ask for less motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Contrast at WCAG AA, keyboard navigation, alt text on every photo, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured, content readable with JavaScript off.
